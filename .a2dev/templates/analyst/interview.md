PRD Interview Flow (Analyst)

Sections
1) Vision & Problem
2) Users & Personas
3) Market & Competitors
4) Value Proposition & Differentiation
5) Scope & MVP
6) Success Metrics (KPIs)
7) Risks & Assumptions
8) Constraints & NFRs
9) Timeline & Milestones

Per‑Section Script
- Ask: 3–5 targeted questions.
- Summarize: draft what will go into PRD.md for this section.
- Confirm: “Change anything? Continue? Or skip next section (NAME)?”

Question Bank

1) Vision & Problem
- What problem are we solving and for whom?
- What triggers this need now? What’s the job-to-be-done?
- What outcomes matter most to the user and business?

2) Users & Personas
- Primary user types? Roles? Decision makers vs. operators?
- Contexts of use (environment, device, frequency)?
- Accessibility needs to anticipate?

3) Market & Competitors
- Top 3 alternatives or competitors today?
- Where do they shine vs. fall short?
- Any regulatory or industry-specific factors?

4) Value Proposition & Differentiation
- What’s your unfair advantage or unique angle?
- Must‑have features vs. nice‑to‑have?
- Proof points (data, customer quotes, benchmarks)?

5) Scope & MVP
- What’s the smallest lovable thing we can ship?
- Must include (features/flows), exclude (out of scope)?
- Key dependencies or integrations?

6) Success Metrics (KPIs)
- Product metrics (activation, retention, NPS, latency, errors)?
- Business metrics (revenue, CAC/LTV, conversion)?
- Target values and measurement plan?

7) Risks & Assumptions
- Biggest technical/product/market risks?
- Unknowns we should validate early?
- Plan B options or feature flags?

8) Constraints & NFRs
- Performance, security, privacy, compliance needs?
- Platforms/browsers/devices to support (min versions)?
- Operational constraints (SLOs, uptime, on-call)?

9) Timeline & Milestones
- Desired launch window and must‑hit dates?
- Internal/external dependencies (teams, vendors, approvals)?
- Beta/GA phases and rollout strategy?

Wrap‑Up
- Present a compiled PRD.md draft for review.
- Offer revisions, then proceed to Backlog/Epics/Stories.
