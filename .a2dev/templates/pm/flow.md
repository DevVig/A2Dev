PM Develop Flow (Conversation Mode)

Phases
1) UX: story goals, flows, edge cases, a11y
2) ADR + NFRs: architecture decisions and non‑functional budgets
3) Deep Plan: tasks, estimates, dependencies
4) QA: scope, test matrix, risks
5) Security: threat model, controls, checks
6) DevOps: build/deploy + release plan + observability
7) Data: events, properties, governance
8) Hypothesis: KPIs, guardrails, experiment design
9) Trace: ACs traceability to artifacts
10) Gate: readiness check and blockers

Per‑Phase Pattern
- Ask 2–4 prompts tailored to the phase.
- Summarize decisions/conclusions.
- Offer: change anything, continue, or skip next (name it).
- Close with 3–5 bullet action items if needed.

Closeout
- Present a consolidated plan and Gate status.
- Propose next owner (Dev or QA) and timeline.
